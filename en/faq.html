DaumBridge – FAQ and troubleshooting

General

Does DaumBridge run on iPhone? No. The blue Daum adapter uses classic Bluetooth (serial connection). Apple only lets apps access that for Apple-certified (MFi) devices – the Daum adapter isn't one. DaumBridge therefore needs an Android phone. The training app itself can run on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac or PC.

Which Daum ergometers are supported? Developed and tested with the Daum 8008 TRS 3. Other Daum models with the same serial protocol may work – feedback is welcome.

The original Daum Bluetooth adapter is sold out – what now? A standard Bluetooth RS232 adapter with classic Bluetooth (SPP) and a DB9 connector works too. What to look for (no BLE version, 9600 baud, connector, null modem): Bluetooth adapters.

Does DaumBridge cost anything? No. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Is the app available in English? Not yet – the app is in German. This documentation explains all menus in English.

Why does it say "Smart" and not "ERG"? Smart is the umbrella term: with Smart ist EIN (smart on) the training app may control the Daum – in ERG mode (fixed watts) or SIM mode (slope), depending on the workout. The Modus (mode) tile shows which one is active.

"Nach Update suchen" (check for update) doesn't work on an older phone (Android 7.0). Up to version 0.9.7, DaumBridge on Android 7.0 (e.g. Galaxy S6) didn't trust GitHub's certificate. Fixed in 0.9.8 – install the new version once via browser or from a PC, after that the update check works again.

Installation

Installing shows “Google Play Store has stopped”. That isn't DaumBridge: when you install an APK, Google Play Protect (part of the Play Store) checks the file, and on older phones that check sometimes crashes. Usually the install still completes – after “Close app”, check the version under menu ☰ → Nach Update suchen (check for updates). Otherwise install again. If that doesn't help: Android settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache, or – if you don't use the Play Store at all – Disable it there (you can Enable it again any time, nothing is deleted).

Do updates delete my settings or rides? No. An update only replaces the app; profiles, settings and saved rides are kept.

Connecting

Android didn't ask for a PIN when pairing – is that wrong? No. Some adapters pair without a PIN. If Android asks, it's the 4-digit number on the adapter.

The "Daum" chip doesn't turn green. - Is the Daum switched on and the adapter plugged in? - Is the adapter paired in the Android Bluetooth settings and selected in DaumBridge under Geräte (devices)? - Is the adapter still connected to another device (old phone, PC)? Disconnect it there. - Switch the phone's Bluetooth off and on, then tap Verbinden (connect) again.

The training app can't find "DaumBridge". - Is a ride running in DaumBridge (stop button visible)? DaumBridge only broadcasts while a ride is running. - Is the BT chip green? - In the training app, search for a smart trainer / controllable trainer, not a power meter. - Laptop: is Bluetooth switched on? Some PCs need a USB Bluetooth dongle.

The training app shows the trainer as "DaumBridge-4874". Some apps (e.g. MyWhoosh) add their own number. That's normal.

Does DaumBridge change my phone's Bluetooth name? Yes, but only during a ride – Android advertises the trainer under the phone's name. After the ride (and after a crash, on the next start) the phone gets its old name back.

Heart rate

DaumBridge shows a far too high heart rate. The Daum's built-in heart rate receiver is often inaccurate. With a Bluetooth heart rate strap (Einstellungen → Geräte → Pulsgurt) DaumBridge only uses the strap's value. DaumBridge ignores the Daum's heart rate entirely: the switch Einstellungen → Geräte → Puls nie vom Daum nehmen (never take heart rate from the Daum) is on by default. Only if you have no strap and still want the Daum's value, turn it off.

The heart rate jumps up and down although I'm sitting still. Usually poor skin contact of the strap: moisten the electrodes, wear the strap tighter right below the chest, avoid synthetic shirts, check the battery. Since 0.9.7 DaumBridge filters gross outliers (above max heart rate, sudden spikes).

Android says "pairing rejected" for the heart rate strap. That's normal – many straps can't be paired. Use Pulsgurt suchen (search strap) in DaumBridge instead.

The heart rate strap doesn't connect. - Put on the strap and moisten the contacts – without skin contact it doesn't send. - Many straps (e.g. Polar H9) allow only one connection. If it's connected to a laptop, watch or training app, disconnect it there. - In DaumBridge go to Einstellungen → Geräte → Pulsgurt and tap Pulsgurt suchen (search strap) – pairing in Android is not needed. - If no values arrive for 20 seconds, DaumBridge reconnects by itself.

The "Zeit in Pulszonen" bar stays empty. It only fills while riding and only when heart rate data arrives. The text next to it says why it's empty ("kein Puls" = no heart rate, "füllt sich beim Fahren" = fills while riding).

Are my heart rate zones right? The automatic calculation estimates max heart rate from age – for many people the real value differs by 10 beats or more. Better: measure your max heart rate (fitness test, sports watch) and enter it. Heart rate zones are not medical advice.

Riding

Turning the knob on the Daum doesn't change the resistance. With Smart ist EIN the training app controls the Daum and overrides the knob. For free riding choose Smart ist AUS.

Climbs are too hard. Lower Einstellungen → Erweitert → Simulatorstärke (e.g. 50–70 %). Or lower Zwift's "trainer difficulty" – but not both by a lot, or it doubles up.

The phone turns off during a ride. Switch on Einstellungen → Erweitert → Bildschirm anlassen (keep screen on). On some phones, also exclude DaumBridge from battery optimisation in the Android settings.

Reporting a problem

  1. Menu ☰ → Protokoll (log) → Teilen (share), e.g. by e-mail or messenger.
  2. Briefly describe what happened and which phone and training app you used.

If DaumBridge crashes, it shows a crash report on the next start, which you can share too.

How do I get my rides onto an iPhone (e.g. into MaxUp)? Easiest with LocalSend: install the free LocalSend app on the iPhone and open it, then pick the iPhone in DaumBridge under Settings → An das iPhone (LocalSend). From then on every ride goes to the iPhone over Wi-Fi automatically when you stop (Files app → LocalSend). Alternatively by e-mail (Settings → Per E-Mail). Bluetooth won't work – iPhones don't accept files from Android phones over Bluetooth.

On one phone the “An … senden” entry is missing / it says “Ziel wählen”. The LocalSend target device is stored per phone. On a phone where none has been chosen yet, the entry reads An iPhone senden – Ziel wählen (send to iPhone – choose target): tap it, pick the iPhone (with LocalSend open) – the ride is sent and the target remembered. Or set it beforehand under Settings → An das iPhone (LocalSend) → Ziel-Gerät.

Can I look at an old ride again? Yes: menu ☰ → Trainings → tap the ride → Diagramm ansehen (view chart). The ride opens in landscape with averages and maximums on the left, the chart of the whole ride on the right and the time in heart-rate zones below. Zoom with two fingers, double-tap shows everything again.

Is the chart included in the FIT file? The data is – power, cadence, heart rate, speed and distance second by second. The picture itself isn't: MaxUp, Strava, Garmin Connect and others draw their own charts on import. The target app calculates heart-rate zones from its own settings.

LocalSend can't find my iPhone. LocalSend has to be open on the iPhone, “Local Network” must be allowed (iPhone Settings → LocalSend), and both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and routers that isolate devices from each other stop them from seeing each other.

LocalSend finds the iPhone but nothing arrives (“Handshake failed” in the log). Older Android phones sometimes can't set up the encrypted connection to LocalSend on the iPhone. Fix: on the iPhone, in LocalSend under Settings, turn Encryption off. DaumBridge detects this by itself and then sends unencrypted – fine on your own Wi-Fi. The iPhone asks whether to accept the file.

Why does the duration show 0:00:00 although I tapped Verbinden (connect)? The clock only starts with your first pedal stroke. With auto-pause (pill at the top right of the duration card) it also stops when you don't pedal for more than 5 seconds.
